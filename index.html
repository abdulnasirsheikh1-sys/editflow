Editing Pipeline

How I Handle Your Project

From raw footage ingestion to final master upload, a 4-step workflow built for precision and speed.

01

Ingestion & Hook Analysis

02

A-Roll Rhythm & Cut

Cutting every millisecond of dead air, awkward pauses, and filler words. Building an uninterrupted rhythm that keeps viewers glued.

03

Sound FX & Motion VFX

Injecting kinetic subtitles in After Effects, impactful sound drops (whooshes, risers, bass impacts), and 4K relevant B-roll cutaways.

Master Export & Drive Upload
